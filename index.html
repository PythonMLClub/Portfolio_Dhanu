Hello 👋 I'm Dhanupriya

Python & AI Developer

Python & AI Developer with 4+ years of experience building scalable backend systems, enterprise AI applications, Generative AI solutions, and computer vision applications. I specialize in Python, FastAPI, Django, PostgreSQL, SQL Server, Snowflake, asynchronous backend development, REST APIs, AI model integration, and enterprise data workflows.

At Inovatrik Technologies (Feb 2026 – Sep 2026), I worked on enterprise AI and backend systems including a multi-tenant ERP/HRMS and payroll platform, a natural-language-to-SQL Smart Query platform, AI image generation, LivePortrait avatar animation, enterprise database exploration and data masking, and MedGemma-based AI services.

My ERP/HRMS work included tenant-isolated PostgreSQL databases, asyncpg-based data access, a zero-ORM payroll calculation engine, safe AST-based formula evaluation, DAG dependency validation, Indian payroll statutory components (EPF, EPS, ESI, PT, LWF), biometric attendance integration, RBAC, secure credential handling, and production deployment workflows.

My Generative AI experience includes integrating and productionizing pretrained Google Gemini, OpenAI GPT/GPT-4o, MedGemma, Snowflake Cortex, and LangChain capabilities for Text-to-SQL, semantic search, AI content generation, conversational applications, document processing, and enterprise analytics.
